## Glossary Terms
- [Vibrating Wire Piezometer \(VWP\)](https://www.advancednavigation.com/glossary/vwp/): Vibrating Wire Piezometer \(VWP\) is an advanced sensor used for precise groundwater pressure measurement in geotechnical and environmental monitoring\.
- [Noise Density](https://www.advancednavigation.com/glossary/noise-density/): Noise density of a sensor may be described as the magnitude of the effects of sensor noise\. The “denser” the sensor noise, the greater the effect or “power” of the noise over a given bandwidth and the greater the variance in sensor output\.
- [Absolute Position](https://www.advancednavigation.com/glossary/absolute-position/): The coordinate position of an object is its latitude \(distance from the equator\), longitude \(distance from the Greenwich meridian in the UK\) and elevation above \(or below\) mean sea level\.
- [Total Positioning Station \(TPS\)](https://www.advancednavigation.com/glossary/tps/): Total Positioning Station \(TPS\) is an advanced surveying instrument that provides precise distance, angle, and coordinate measurements for mapping and construction\.
- [Ground Penetrating Radar \(GPR\)](https://www.advancednavigation.com/glossary/gpr/): Ground Penetrating Radar \(GPR\) is a non\-invasive technology that uses radar pulses to detect subsurface objects and structures with precision\.
